EDF

Before we fix a system, we prove what is wrong

  • What EDF does

    A structured approach to understanding engineering performance across architecture, delivery, and decision-making.

  • Why it matters

    Evidence, constraints, trade-offs, and targeted interventions instead of vague maturity talk.

  • 01. Understand the domain

    Map stakeholders, constraints, and systems.

  • 02. Identify symptom patterns

    Collect signals until failure clusters appear.

  • 03. Trace causal mechanisms

    Explain why the problem reproduces.

  • 04. Classify the pathology

    Separate design, process, and control failures.

  • 05. Deliver treatment

    Stabilize, change governance, and keep the record.
